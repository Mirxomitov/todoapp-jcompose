## Full description
Mir Tasks is a clean, fast to-do list that keeps your day organized without accounts, ads or internet.

• Add tasks in seconds, with an optional note
• Set a due date: today, tomorrow or any day you pick
• Mark priority as low, medium or high and see it at a glance
• Smart views: All, Today (including overdue), Upcoming and Done
• Overdue tasks are highlighted so nothing slips through
• Swipe to delete, with undo if you change your mind
• Light and dark themes
• Available in English, O'zbekcha and Русский

Private by design: your tasks are stored only on your phone. Mir Tasks has no internet permission, no analytics and no ads.
